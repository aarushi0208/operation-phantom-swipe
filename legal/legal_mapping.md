# 1. ATM SKIMMING

## Information Technology Act, 2000

### Section 43

Section 43 deals with unauthorized acts involving a computer,
computer system or computer network, including unauthorized access,
copying or extraction of data and related forms of interference.

It may be relevant where digital systems or data are accessed or
copied without authorization.

### Section 66

Section 66 applies where an act referred to in Section 43 is done
dishonestly or fraudulently.

Therefore, where unauthorized computer-related activity is carried
out as part of a fraudulent scheme, Section 66 may become relevant.

# 2. CREDIT CARD CLONING

## Information Technology Act, 2000

### Section 66C — Identity Theft

Section 66C addresses fraudulent or dishonest use of another person's
electronic signature, password or other unique identification
feature.

It may be relevant where stolen digital identity or authentication
information is misused.

### Section 66D — Cheating by Personation Using Computer Resource

Section 66D addresses cheating by personation using a communication
device or computer resource.

It may apply where stolen identity or payment information is used
through computer resources to impersonate another person.

# 5. IMPORTANT CURRENT-LAW NOTE

The IPC is no longer the principal criminal code for offences
committed after the new criminal laws came into force.

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha
Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 came
into force from 1 July 2024.

Therefore, this assignment's IPC references are included because the
assignment specifically requests IPC, while a real current
investigation would need to examine the applicable BNS provisions
and other current legislation.

# 6. BUDAPEST CONVENTION

The Budapest Convention on Cybercrime provides an international
framework for cybercrime and electronic evidence.

Relevant areas include:

- Illegal access
- Data and system interference
- Computer-related fraud
- Computer-related forgery
- Procedural measures for electronic evidence
- International cooperation

For this scenario, the most relevant area is computer-related fraud
and the preservation and investigation of electronic evidence.

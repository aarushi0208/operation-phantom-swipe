## 📌 Project Overview

Operation Phantom Swipe is a simulated digital-forensics
investigation involving a cross-border ATM skimming and online
credit-card fraud scenario.

The project demonstrates foundational digital-forensics procedures
including evidence acquisition, chain of custody, cryptographic
hashing, digital artefact analysis, password-recovery simulation,
cybercrime classification and legal analysis.

All evidence used in this project is synthetic and was created only
for academic purposes.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Classify the cybercrimes involved in the scenario.
- Simulate seizure and acquisition of electronic evidence.
- Maintain a chain of custody.
- Generate SHA-256 hashes for evidence files.
- Search and analyse simulated digital artefacts.
- Perform controlled dictionary-based password recovery.
- Study legal and ethical considerations.
- Examine challenges in cross-border cybercrime investigations.
- Document the complete investigation process.

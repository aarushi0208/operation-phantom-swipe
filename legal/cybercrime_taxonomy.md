# CYBERCRIME TAXONOMY

## Case

Operation Phantom Swipe

## Case ID

OPS-PS-2026-001

---

## 1. ATM Skimming

### Classification

ATM skimming is a form of financial cybercrime and payment-card
data theft.

### Description

In the simulated scenario, a skimming device is used to capture
payment-card information from an ATM interaction.

### Cybercrime Category

Primary Category:
Financial Cybercrime

Secondary Categories:
- Data Theft
- Payment Card Fraud
- Unauthorized Data Collection
- Cyber-enabled Theft

### Digital Footprint

Potential evidence includes:

- Skimmer configuration files
- Device logs
- Captured card references
- Timestamps
- Device identifiers
- Location information
- Communication records

---

## 2. Credit Card Cloning

### Classification

Credit-card cloning is a form of identity-related financial fraud
in which stolen payment-card information is reproduced or used to
conduct unauthorized transactions.

### Cybercrime Category

Primary Category:
Financial Cybercrime

Secondary Categories:
- Identity Theft
- Payment Fraud
- Unauthorized Use of Credentials
- Data Misuse

### Digital Footprint

Potential evidence includes:

- Card-data records
- Transaction information
- Device logs
- Authentication records
- Messages between suspects
- Browser history
- Payment-related applications

---

## 3. Online Credit Card Fraud

### Classification

Online credit-card fraud is a computer-enabled financial crime in
which stolen or fraudulent payment information is used to obtain
money, goods or services without authorization.

### Cybercrime Category

Primary Category:
Computer-related Fraud

Secondary Categories:
- Financial Fraud
- Identity Theft
- Online Personation
- Unauthorized Transactions

### Digital Footprint

Potential evidence includes:

- Browser history
- Email messages
- IP addresses
- Login records
- Transaction records
- Device identifiers
- Application data
- Communication records

# Justification

The crimes are classified as cybercrime because digital devices,
electronic payment systems, computer resources and electronically
stored information are used as tools, targets or sources of evidence.

The investigation therefore requires both conventional criminal
investigation techniques and digital forensic examination.
